# Aurora retro — 2026-09-18

Present: Iven, Mira, Tomas. Two weeks after the revision B board went up on the
balcony. Timebox: 45 minutes, no slides.

## What went well

- The sensor head survived two storms and a wasp nest without a leak. The
  gasket channel was worth the extra print time.
- The power budget held: the first fortnight averaged 9.4 mAh/day against a
  predicted 9.52. Being slightly under is a first.
- Shipping the Parquet writer before the dashboard meant history existed on day
  one, so the first dashboard build had real data to draw.

## What did not

- **Wi-Fi reconnect storms.** Two days of association failures cost 120 mAh
  before anyone noticed, because the dashboard showed battery voltage only
  every six hours. Retry cap is now three, then sleep.
- **The antenna.** −78 dBm with the balcony door closed. The PCB antenna is
  tuned for free space and the door frame is a metal box. This is the winter
  case and it is unresolved.
- **Firmware build time.** 1.8 GB of ESP-IDF for a 300-line program still feels
  wrong. Nobody had a better idea; parked as "known tax".
- **Calibration was manual.** Three units, three offset sheets, done by hand at
  23:00 the night before installation. Should be a script.
